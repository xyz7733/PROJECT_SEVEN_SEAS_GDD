# Audio sources and licences

Every sound in Project Seven Seas falls into one of two groups:

- **Third-party, CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication).** It is listed below with its source page, creator, original filename and the licence wording shown on the page.
- **Original to this project.** It was synthesised by `tools/audiogen/gen_stingers.py` (the stingers), `tools/audiogen/gen_weather.py` (the rain and storm beds, thunder and the boss horn), or `tools/audiogen/gen_regional.py` (ten regional layers and one cabin layer) and is released under CC0 1.0 with the project.

Selection rules:

- Kenney packs were preferred.
- OpenGameArt and BigSoundBank items were used only after their pages were checked one by one.
- CC0 items were kept.
- Anything non-commercial (NC), no-derivatives (ND), ambiguous or of unclear provenance was rejected. The rejected items are listed at the end.

None of these licences requires attribution. The in-game credits line and this file credit the creators anyway.

The full CC0 1.0 legal code ships as `licenses/CC0-1.0-legalcode.txt`. The Kenney pack licence notes ship as `licenses/kenney_*.txt`. In the source tree, both are in `game/assets/audio/licenses/`.

### Original weather beds (Project Seven Seas, CC0 1.0)

| Game file | What it is |
|---|---|
| `rain_loop.wav` | 8 s of rain: hiss and body noise shaped in the frequency domain, a slow swell, a few hundred tiny droplets (bubbles whose pitch rises as they ring down) and some heavier drops on a deck. Generated by `tools/audiogen/gen_weather.py` (fixed random seed). |
| `storm_loop.wav` | 10 s of storm: heavier rain in gusts, wind moaning through rigging (three narrow resonances that swell apart) and a low rumble. Same generator. |

Both are exact loops: the noise is periodic over the file, the droplets wrap round the end and every swell completes a whole number of cycles. They play on top of the stage's own bed (sea, port or island) while it rains, crossfaded over 2.5 s when the weather changes, and not underground.

`Audio.scene()` selects a quiet regional layer through the existing Ambience bus. It is attenuated
in battle and suppressed in dungeon/secret interiors. Ship deck selection changes it to the cabin
layer below decks and muffles the exterior field-recording bed. It returns to the region outside.
The layers complement the existing music, stage ambience and weather, rather than replace them.

`regional/manifest.json` records each seed, duration, sample rate, channel count, peak/RMS level and
boundary-step measurement. Those are numerical checks of generated files. Human listening,
speaker mixing, loop-seam assessment and shelter transitions remain pending for world-art-01;
the metadata does not establish an audible pass. Native resource import and Windows release export
completed; the package audit includes all eleven new loops. Runtime listening remains unverified.

### Artwork provenance is separate

The CC0 declarations in this document concern audio. Most game art is original code-authored voxel
or pixel artwork generated by `tools/artgen/`. World-art-01 also introduces ten AI-assisted regional
landmark masters created with OpenAI ImageGen under `art/source/landmarks/`, prepared for a game
atlas by `tools/artgen/gen_landmarks.py`. Their exact tool model is not asserted. Retained prompts,
source/preparation metadata and the final atlas inventory identify that contribution separately;
these images are not covered by the audio's CC0 dedication. See `docs/WORLD_ART_01.md` and
`docs/ASSET_INVENTORY.md` for artwork provenance and integration status.

## Where each effect is used

The effect table is defined in `game/scripts/core/audio.gd` under `SFX` and `TX`.

UI:

- Buttons click.
- Primary buttons confirm.
- Cancel and close buttons go "back" or "close".
- Toggles and tabs switch.
- Errors play the error cue.
- Windows open and close with their own cues.

Ship:

| Action | Sound |
|---|---|
| Place, move or add stairs | Wood thud |
| Demolish | Plank knock |
| Repair or upgrade | Light wood taps |
| Door | Door open |
| Hull refit | Creak |
| Level switch | Creak |

Trade, recruiting and quests:

| Action | Sound |
|---|---|
| Buy, sell, invest or recruit | Coins |
| Quests and records | Page flip |

Voyage:

| Moment | Sound |
|---|---|
| Departure | Bell and sail cloth |
| Arrival | Bell |
| Rowboat landing | Rowing |
| Lightning in a storm | `thunder_near` or `thunder_far`, after a delay that grows with the distance of the strike |

Battle:

| Event | Sound |
|---|---|
| Melee | Swing or slash, then punch hits |
| Critical or boss hits | Heavy hit |
| Knockout | Soft heavy impact |
| Skill | Hit jingle |
| Heal | Softer jingle |
| Gunner shots | `cannon_fire` at low volume and pitch jitter |
| Wave | Bell |
| Boarding start | `ship_ram` |
| Hazards | Cannon hit or splash |
| Victory or defeat | Stinger, with the music ducked |
| A boss steps forward | `boss_horn`, with the music ducked |
| Retreat | Minimize cue and cloth |

How repeats are kept restrained:

- Each effect has a cooldown and a cap on how many copies play at once.
- Variants rotate, so the same take does not play twice in a row.
- Pitch jitter is at most ±8 %.
- Twelve effect voices are shared, and the oldest is stolen when all are busy.

## Volume

The System window has four sliders: Master, Music, Ambience and Effects.

- They drive the `Master`, `Music`, `Ambience` and `SFX` buses, which the game creates at start.
- Values are stored in `user://settings.cfg`.
- 0 % mutes a bus.
